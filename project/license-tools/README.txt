IR NDA APP v1.2.8 - LICENSE GENERATOR

1. Open IR_NDA_LICENSE_GENERATOR.html in Chrome on your phone.
2. Ask the customer to open IR NDA APP. The first screen shows a Device ID.
3. Customer taps COPY DEVICE ID and sends it to you on WhatsApp.
4. Paste the Device ID into the generator.
5. Choose validity: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 180 days, 1 year, or Lifetime.
6. Tap GENERATE LICENSE KEY.
7. Tap COPY KEY FOR WHATSAPP and send the key to the customer.
8. Customer enters the key in the app and taps ACTIVATE APP.

The key is bound to the Device ID and contains a signed expiry date. No server is required.
Keep the generator private. Do not send it to customers.
